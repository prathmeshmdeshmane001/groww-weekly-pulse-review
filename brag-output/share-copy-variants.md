## LinkedIn
Product teams often drown in thousands of app store reviews without knowing which bugs are actually hurting retention.

I built **Groww Weekly Pulse Review** — an end-to-end customer feedback intelligence system:
• Ingests live Google Play Store & Apple App Store reviews
• Enforces Privacy-by-Design with two-pass PII redaction
• Clusters friction themes & weights 1★/2★ pain using Gemini
• Enforces a hard validation gate: ≤ 250 words, 3 themes, 3 verbatim quotes, 3 grounded actions
• Delivers scannable briefs straight to Google Docs and Gmail via Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Live Dashboard: https://groww-weekly-pulse-review.vercel.app
Backend API: https://groww-weekly-pulse-api.onrender.com
