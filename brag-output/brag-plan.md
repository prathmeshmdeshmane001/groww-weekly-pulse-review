# Brag Plan: Groww Weekly Pulse Review

## What is this app?
An automated AI customer feedback intelligence engine and executive dashboard for Groww that ingests live App Store and Play Store reviews, scrubs PII at ingestion, clusters themes using Gemini, enforces a strict ≤250 word limit, and delivers weekly pulses directly to Google Docs and Gmail via MCP.

## The angle
Fintech moves at breakneck speed, but product and growth teams drown in thousands of unstructured app store reviews every week. Groww Weekly Pulse turns 2,000+ noisy store reviews into a scannable, validated ≤250-word executive brief in Google Docs and Gmail in minutes—zero PII leakage, zero manual API plumbing.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography on dark slate with Groww emerald glow: "2,041 app reviews dropped this week. Who's reading all of them?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- Autonomous Multi-Store Ingestion: Google Play + Apple App Store with two-pass PII scrubbing (emails, phones, usernames completely redacted).
- Gemini AI Theme Clustering & Severity Ranking: Surfacing top friction themes (Charges & Fees, App Performance, Payments) weighted by 1★/2★ pain.
- Hard Validation Gate & MCP Delivery: Enforcing ≤ 250 words, exactly 3 themes, 3 verbatim quotes, 3 grounded actions, published directly to Google Docs and Gmail Drafts.

## Outro / punchline
"Signal extracted. Noise deleted. Ready for leadership."
Final logo lockup with Groww emerald icon, product name, and live deployment badge.

## User flow worth showing
Select date range / click "Generate New Pulse" → Real-time multi-store scraping & PII redaction → Gemini theme clustering & 250-word validation → One-click Google Docs & Gmail deliverable created.
